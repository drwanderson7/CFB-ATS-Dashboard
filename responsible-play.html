Responsible Play

Last updated: August 2026

PickGauge is built for friendly pick'em pools and sports analysis — it is not a sportsbook, and it doesn't process or hold wagers. Some pools can still involve money or prizes, so use PickGauge only if you're of legal age where you live and your participation follows applicable laws and the pool organizer's rules.

If picking games has stopped being fun — if you're thinking about it more than you'd like, spending more time or money on it than you intended, or picking to chase a bad week rather than because you enjoy it — that's worth paying attention to, and help is available.

1-800-MY-RESET
National Problem Gambling Helpline · call, text, or chat · free, confidential, 24/7
Also reachable at 1-800-522-4700. For online chat and help resources, visit 1800myreset.org

A few things worth checking in on

  • Are you setting a limit before picking, and sticking to it?
  • Are you picking because it's fun, or to try to win back a bad week?
  • Is the time you spend on pools taking away from other things you care about?
  • Would it bother you if someone close to you saw how much time or money you'd put into this?

None of that is a diagnosis — just questions worth asking yourself honestly once in a while. If the answers give you pause, the helpline above is a good next step, whether it's for you or someone you're worried about.
