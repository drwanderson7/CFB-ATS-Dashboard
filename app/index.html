Backup

Everything saves in this browser automatically, and syncs to your account too if you're signed in. Export a file to move it or keep a copy.

If you're signed in, importing a backup asks for confirmation, then atomically restores it to your account — every signed-in device will see it. If you're not signed in, it only replaces what's stored in this browser; your account (if you have one) is untouched.

"Reset this browser" only clears what's stored locally here — if you're signed in, it does not touch your synced account data, which can pull back down the next time this browser syncs. Export a backup first if you actually want to keep a copy before clearing.

Delete my data

Permanently deletes everything synced to your account — every pool, pick, entry, and archived week, on every signed-in device. This is separate from your sign-in itself (email/password/session), which you manage from your account menu.

This can't be undone. Export a backup first (above) if you might want a copy. Not signed in? There's nothing on the server to delete — use "Reset this browser" above instead.

Glossary

Key PickGauge terms

PickGauge Model #

PickGauge's default projected spread. It blends five selected prediction models with the live market and becomes available when the market plus at least three of those models are available.

How PickGauge Model # works →
Edge

The point difference between the active model projection and the line you are picking against. In a pool, that means the pool's locked line—not today's live market.

Edge & Cover % methodology →
Cover %

PickGauge's estimated probability that the recommended side covers the listed spread, based on historical FBS cover-margin behavior.

How Cover % is estimated →
Strong / Good / Slim

These labels describe the size of the model-vs-line gap. Strong clears the higher edge threshold, Good clears the normal edge threshold, and Slim means the model and the line barely disagree. They describe edge size, not a guarantee.

Market vs. locked pool line

Market is the current sportsbook spread. A locked pool line is the contest number your pick will actually be graded against. Pool Edge uses the locked line; Market remains visible for comparison.

CLV

Closing Line Value. In pool views, CLV shows how the live market has moved from the line your contest locked. Once you pick a side, positive CLV means the movement improved the value of the number you already have.

CLV methodology →
My Numbers

Your own projected spreads. They are saved separately from PickGauge Model # so you can compare your opinion with PickGauge and the market without silently changing the default model.

Custom Blend

An optional user-controlled blend of PickGauge Model # and any additional models or line inputs you enable. When active, Custom Blend drives Edge and Cover % while the standalone PickGauge Model # remains visible unchanged. Turn PickGauge Model # off to build a fully custom Model # from only the models and inputs you choose.

How Custom Blend works →
Brad Powers / Computer Line

Two projected spreads imported from the Powers newsletter. They can be shown for comparison or intentionally added to a Custom Blend.

Common tasks

How do I…?

Refresh market lines

Open All Games and use Refresh lines. Signed-in users can use PickGauge's shared odds connection automatically; a personal Odds API key in Settings is optional.

Create or update an ATS pool

Open All Games → Pools. Create the pool once, then update that week's contest lines by importing a supported sheet/PDF or entering lines manually.

Customize the models

Open All Games → More → Models & weights. Keep PickGauge Model # as the default, turn on other models for comparison, or assign weights to build a Custom Blend.

Add my own projections

Open All Games → More → My Numbers. Enter projected spreads directly or import a CSV. My Numbers stay separate from PickGauge Model #.

Understand why PickGauge likes a mobile matchup

On mobile, open Why [team]? on the game card to reveal model agreement, key-number context, My Numbers, CLV when applicable, and Matchup Intelligence.

Back up or restore my data

Open Settings. Export creates a JSON backup. Import/Restore replaces the appropriate saved state after confirmation; signed-in account data syncs across your devices.
