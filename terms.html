What this is

PickGauge is a decision-support tool for against-the-spread pick'em pools. It pulls in market lines, prediction-system data, and numbers you provide, and surfaces them in one place so you can compare them and track your own picks. It is not a sportsbook, does not facilitate wagering, and does not process or hold any money on your behalf.

Sports outcomes are inherently uncertain; a favorable "edge" reading is a statistical lean based on historical data, not a prediction you should treat as certain.
