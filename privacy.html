Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

Edge Board is a small, independently-run tool for tracking against-the-spread picks in college football pick'em pools. This page explains what information the app collects, how it's used, and what control you have over it.

Account information

Signing in is handled entirely by Clerk, a third-party authentication provider. Edge Board never sees or stores your password — Clerk manages that directly. The only account information Edge Board itself uses is your Clerk user ID (an anonymous identifier, not your email) and, where you've provided one, your email address for sign-in purposes. See Clerk's own privacy policy for how they handle sign-in data.

What Edge Board stores about your use of the tool

Once you're signed in, the following is saved to a private database bucket tied to your account, and is not visible to any other user:

  • Your picks, pool entries, and which pools you've joined
  • Pool sheets you've imported (game lines, pick limits, entry names)
  • Prediction-input data you've imported or entered (e.g. from a Powers PDF), and which prediction systems and weights you've enabled

Separately, a small amount of data is shared across every signed-in user of this deployment — current game lines, prediction-system averages, and the structure of any pool someone has published (games, pick limits, entry names). Your individual picks and entries are never included in that shared data — only the pool's structure is shared, so others can join and pick within it.

Your Odds API key stays on your device. If you add a personal key from The Odds API in Settings, it's stored only in your browser's local storage. It is never sent to or stored on Edge Board's servers, and it's explicitly excluded from account sync and from any backup file you export.

Other services this tool talks to

Edge Board is built on a few third-party services, each governed by its own privacy policy:

  • Clerk — authentication and sign-in
  • Vercel — hosting for the app and its backend functions
  • Upstash — the database that stores your synced picks/pools/inputs
  • The Odds API and thepredictiontracker.com — sources for game lines and prediction-system data (no personal information is sent to either)

Exporting and deleting your data

Export: Settings → Export backup (.json) downloads everything synced to your account as a plain JSON file, anytime.

Deletion: Edge Board doesn't yet have a self-serve "delete my account" button. To have your account and all associated data permanently removed, contact us and we'll take care of it. We're not going to pretend a button exists that doesn't — this page will be updated if that changes.

Cookies and tracking

Edge Board itself doesn't use analytics or advertising cookies. Clerk sets its own session cookies to keep you signed in; see their policy for details.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes in a meaningful way, the "last updated" date above will change too.
