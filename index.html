Stop bouncing between PDFs, prediction systems, sportsbook lines, spreadsheets, and pool pages. PickGauge brings the decision-making layer together.

01

Model vs. Market

Compare your projected line against the current or pool spread and surface the games with the largest ATS disagreement.

Bring your strongest weekly edges to the top so you can focus your research where it matters most.

SYS_PROCESS: Workflow

From raw inputs to a seven-pick card.

The workflow is built around the way ATS pool players actually make decisions each week.

STEP_01

Import your pool

Upload a PDF of your Splash Sports or ESPN Pick'em sheet—or manually select games for any other pool—and PickGauge builds your board against the pool's actual locked lines, not just the live market.

STEP_02

Build your model

Choose from 20 of the best computer prediction systems, set your own weights, and build the composite line that's actually yours.

STEP_03

Find the disagreement

PickGauge calculates the difference between your model and the line, then highlights the strongest opportunities.

STEP_04

Build your entries

Select your favorite ATS plays, manage multiple entries, and preserve the line you actually picked.
