How to use Edge board

Quick start

  1. Go to Settings, paste your free API key from the-odds-api.com, click Save key.
  2. Click ↻ Refresh lines (top right). The board fills with this week's NCAAF games and live spreads.
  3. The numbers in My # are: BP and Comp (auto-filled from the Powers PDF), any prediction systems you've toggled on in Settings (auto-filled from thepredictiontracker.com via ⬇ Load predictions), and the live Vegas line. My # is the straight average of whichever are filled.
  4. Click ⬆ Import Powers PDF and pick that week's Powers newsletter — BP and Comp fill in automatically for every game it matches.
  5. Click ⇅ Re-sort by edge to push the biggest edges to the top.
  6. In My picks, add a pool entry. Back on the board, pick the entry from Picking for, then star your favorite games (max 7) for that entry.
  7. Repeat the star step per entry. Review them all under My picks.

What each column means

  • Game — away @ home, with kickoff time.
  • BP — Brad Powers' line, auto-filled from the Powers PDF (page 2), converted to home-team perspective.
  • Comp — the computer projected line, auto-filled from the Powers PDF (page 6), home perspective.
  • Prediction systems — any computer systems you've enabled in Settings → Prediction systems, pulled from thepredictiontracker.com. Each toggled system adds one number to the average.
  • Vegas ● — the live home-team spread (book shown beneath), averaged into My # along with BP, Comp, and your toggled systems.
  • My # — straight average of every filled number (BP, Comp, each toggled system, live Vegas).
  • Edge — pick — the side to take, the market number for that side, and the points of value in the pill.

How the edge is read

  • Edge = the gap between My # and the live line. It also tells you which side that gap favors and the number you'd actually bet.
  • Green = the gap clears your threshold (set in Settings, default 1.5). Deep green = a strong edge (default ≥ 3).
  • Red = the gap is below threshold — your number basically agrees with the market, so no real edge.

FAQ

Do I need IT or admin rights?

No. The API key is a free personal signup. Everything runs in your browser and saves locally.

Will it use up my free API calls?

Only when you click Refresh lines. One click = one pull of all games. The remaining count shows top-right after each refresh.

Where do my picks and numbers go?

They're stored in this browser (localStorage). Use Settings → Export to back them up or move browsers.

How does the Powers import work?

It reads page 2 (BP line) and page 6 (Comp line) of the Powers newsletter, converts both to home-team perspective, and matches each game to your board by team name. Games it can't match are counted next to the button and logged to the browser console so you can fill those by hand.

How do the prediction systems work?

In Settings → Prediction systems, toggle on any of the ~40 computer systems tracked at thepredictiontracker.com (Sagarin, ESPN FPI, Donchess, Dokter, Big 200, and more). Hit ⬇ Load predictions on the board and each enabled system's line for that week gets averaged into My # for every game it covers. Your toggle choices sync across devices.

Why is a game missing?

The board shows what the books currently list for NCAAF. Games far out, or off the board, may not appear until closer to kickoff.
